Bantay Map Prototype • Operator Manual

System Guide: Buttons, Sections, Symbols & Legends

Crossing Operational States (Color Legend)

CLEAR (Green)
No train approaching. Dual 3.5m fiberglass barrier arms are raised upright; road traffic may cross safely.
PRE_ALERT (Amber)
2-of-3 sensors confirmed an approaching train (T-33s to T-26s). 3 Hz LED strobes and 90 dB chimes warn pedestrians and motorists.
GATE_LOWERED (Crimson Red)
Triggered at T-25s before arrival. Entrance arm lowers first, followed by exit arm (+3s stagger) so no vehicle is trapped inside.
POST_PASS_HOLD (Orange • 5.0s)
After the locomotive passes, the barrier stays closed for an extra 5.0 seconds to ensure the last coach clears before reopening.
MANUAL (PNR Cobalt Blue)
Local Riles Marshal used the physical emergency key switch at the Bantay Post to control the drop-arm manually.
FAULT / OFFLINE
FAULT: Motor overcurrent obstacle reversal or enclosure tamper switch triggered. OFFLINE: GSM cellular heartbeat missed.

Toggles the weekly vandalism/debris heat overlay on the map. QR Incident Form opens the mobile-first anonymized reporting form (RA 10173 compliant) used by train crews and barangay spotters.

Shadow Mode runs full sensor detection and telemetry logging while keeping physical drop-arms upright during the 30-day calibration phase. Override lets operators manually test states like FAULT, OFFLINE, or MANUAL.
